What driving is worth

Used to rank airports on what the whole trip costs rather than the fare alone. The hourly figure is what an hour behind the wheel is worth to you — not your salary, since a drive was never billable. The mileage figure is fuel and wear, not the IRS rate, which folds in costs you pay whether the car moves or not.

About the numbers

Award charts, transfer ratios, and welcome bonuses in this app were last reviewed . They change without notice, and several programs (United, Delta, Flying Blue) publish no chart at all — those are derived from the cash fare.

Point transfers are irreversible. Always confirm the live award price on the airline's own site before moving any points. This app tells you where to look; it cannot see whether an award seat is actually available.

Edit data/charts.js, data/programs.js, or data/cards.js to correct any value — they're plain files, no build step.

All data stays on this device.
